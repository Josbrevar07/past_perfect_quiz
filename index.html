Past Perfect Tense Quiz

Complete each sentence using the correct Past Perfect form of the verbs in parentheses.

When you finish the exercises, click the blue check button to review your answers. Incorrect answer will turn red. Correct answer will turn green.

1. By the time she arrived, we (eat) dinner.
2. He (finish) the book before the deadline.
3. They (not/see) that movie before last night.
4. I (never/hear) that song before today.
5. We (live) in Paris before we moved to Berlin.
6. She (not/do) her homework when the teacher collected it.
